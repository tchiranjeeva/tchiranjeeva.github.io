An ATmega328P based medication adherence device with programmable alarm
scheduling and real time clock integration for elderly care.

<!--more-->

<img src="{{ '/assets/img/projects/medi-box-render.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="medi box board render">

## Overview

- **MCU:** ATmega328P
- **Features:** Programmable alarm scheduling, real time clock, user friendly
  interface
- **Tools:** EasyEDA, Arduino, Fritzing, Tinkercad, multimeter

## Details

Built to help with medication adherence in elderly care settings.The RTC
drives programmable alarm schedules so reminders trigger at the right dose
times, with a simple interface aimed at users who aren't comfortable with
more complex devices.
