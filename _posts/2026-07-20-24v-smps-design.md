A Switch Mode Power Supply built around the ST VIPER38 switching IC,
delivering a stable 24 V output at 1 A.

<!--more-->

## Overview

- **Topology:** Switch-mode, ST VIPER38 controller IC
- **Output:** 24 V, 1 A
- **Tools:** Eagle, oscilloscope, multimeter, variac

## Details

Optimized for efficiency and thermal performance in a compact form factor —
the VIPER38's integrated switch simplifies the design while still hitting a
stable 24 V/1 A output under load. Bench-verified with a variac and
oscilloscope across the input range to confirm regulation and thermal
behavior.
