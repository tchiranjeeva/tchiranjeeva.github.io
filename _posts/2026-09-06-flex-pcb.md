A flexible LED strip PCB with integrated capacitive touch control and

## Overview

- **Form factor:** Flexible PCB
- **Touch:** Eight capacitive touch pads on a dedicated touch sensor
- **Lighting:** Addressable RGB LEDs
- **Interface:** FPC edge connector to a separate controller board

## Details

The flexible construction and FPC edge connector make this well suited to
compact or wearable lighting applications. Eight capacitive touch pads,
wired to a dedicated touch sensor IC, allow interactive control of the
lighting effects directly from the strip itself, while the addressable RGB
LEDs handle the actual illumination — all driven from a separate controller
board over the FPC connector.
