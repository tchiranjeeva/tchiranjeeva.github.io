A compact circular LED indicator board designed for automotive electrical environments. A low dropout voltage regulator converts the vehicle supply into a stable 9 V rail for the LEDs, and a TVS diode protects the board from the voltage spikes and transients common in vehicles.

Five LEDs (red, green and blue among them) are arranged around the centre, each with its own current limiting resistor. The round shape and central mounting hole let it fit into circular housings, and the VDD and GND solder pads make wiring simple.

<img src="{{ '/assets/img/projects/circular-led-board.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Automotive Circular LED Indicator Board 3D Render">

### Key Features

- **Automotive-grade input voltage conditioning** handling harsh vehicular 12V/24V spikes
- **Onboard low-dropout (LDO) regulator** generating a stable 9 V internal LED rail
- **TVS diode protection** against load-dump and inductive transients
- **Multi-color LED cluster** (RGB / indicator LEDs) with independent ballast resistors
- **Circular form factor** with central mounting hole for panel gauges and enclosures
- **Generous solder pads** for reliable field harness connection
