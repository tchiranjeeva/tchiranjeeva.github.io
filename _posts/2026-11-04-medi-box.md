A smart medicine carriage and schedule reminder system built with an ATmega328p MCU, featuring customizable audible alarms, push-button controls, and LCD display.

<!--more-->

The Medi Box is a smart medicine carriage equipped with an ATmega328p microcontroller that ensures timely medication reminders through an audible alarm. Users can customize alarm settings for specific times and dates using four push buttons, offering flexibility for various medication schedules.

The alarm prompts users to take their medicine and requires manual deactivation, reinforcing adherence. Designed with a user-friendly interface, the Medi Box integrates seamlessly into daily routines. Its 2-layer PCB, created using EasyEDA, reflects a compact and efficient design, making it a reliable solution for medication management.

<img src="{{ '/assets/img/projects/medi-box-board.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="Medi Box Fabricated PCB Assembled">

### Key Features

- **ATmega328p** microcontroller core
- **RTC and customizable alarm scheduling** for multi-dose daily regimens
- **Audible buzzer alarm** requiring manual user confirmation to silence
- **4-button control panel** with 16x2 character LCD display
- **Compact 2-layer PCB layout** designed in EasyEDA
- **Dual power capability:** 9V battery or regulated DC adapter
